== The Examples menu

image::ui-examples-menu.png[The Examples menu on the canvas toolbar]

btn:[Examples] lists every ready-made definition: the twenty-eight above, the tutorial definitions and the tolerance examples. Choosing one replaces what is on the canvas, so save first if you want to keep your own work. Each loads in a second or two; the preview then updates as you drag.

These pictures come from the same definitions. The red preview is what you see while you work; the colours come from baking the final components and setting their colour in FreeCAD, which is how you would prepare a render of your own.

== What they teach

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Example |New ideas
|xref:tutorial-voronoi.adoc[Voronoi screen] |*Populate 2D*, *Voronoi*, *Area* centroids, scaling about a per-item centre, cutting many holes with one *Solid Difference*
|xref:tutorial-tower.adoc[Twisted tower] |One item per floor from *Series*, a shaping formula in *Expression*, *Regular Polygon*, *Loft* with Flatten, *Planar Surface* slabs
|xref:tutorial-waffle.adoc[Waffle dome] |A profile curve from a formula, *Cap Holes* to close a loft, *Section* with a list of offsets, centring a series, thickening flat regions
|xref:tutorial-wave.adoc[Wave surface] |A 2D grid with Graft, a formula of two variables, *Surface From Points*, *Contour* at a list of heights
|xref:tutorial-phyllotaxis.adoc[Sunflower] |Positions from formulas of an item's number, *Sphere* and *Move* with lists, the golden angle
|xref:tutorial-hyperboloid.adoc[Shukhov tower] |*Divide Curve*, *Shift List* to pair offset points, *Line*, *Evaluate Curve* with Graft, a periodic *Interpolate*, *Pipe*
|xref:tutorial-staircase.adoc[Spiral staircase] |*Rotate* with a list of angles, *Cylinder*, cos/sin positions, a rail interpolated through baluster tops
|xref:tutorial-city.adoc[Voronoi city] |Plots and streets with *Scale*, a Gaussian height formula, repeatable noise from *Deconstruct* and *Expression*
|xref:tutorial-weave.adoc[Woven mat] |Two families from one grafted tree, over-under with `cos(π(i + j))`, free curve ends, *Pipe* along S-curves
|xref:tutorial-knot.adoc[Torus knot] |A parametric space curve, a periodic *Interpolate*, *Pipe* along a closed 3D curve
|xref:tutorial-ring.adoc[Twisted lobed ring] |A profile per branch, lobes from `cos(L·θ)`, *Loft* with *Closed*
|xref:tutorial-frames.adoc[Frame tunnel] |One Boolean, many copies: *Scale*, *Rotate* about a centre, *Move* with lists
|xref:tutorial-hypar.adoc[Hypar umbrella] |Doubly ruled surfaces, *Ruled Surface*, copies by grafting *Rotate*'s angles
|xref:tutorial-slats.adoc[Slatted wave ceiling] |A phase per branch, *Ruled Surface* between paired curves, boards from *Extrude*
|xref:tutorial-monolith.adoc[Carved monolith] |An attractor driving solids: *Sphere* sizes from *Remap*, one *Solid Difference* with a list
|xref:tutorial-flange.adoc[Flange with tolerance zones] |Geometry and tolerance together: *Bonus Tolerance*, *Virtual & Resultant Condition*, a *Panel*
|xref:tutorial-bridge.adoc[Network-arch bridge] |*Arc 3Pt*, *Evaluate Curve* at offset parameters, *Mirror*
|xref:tutorial-shell.adoc[Spiral shell] |A tangent from a derivative, *Circle CNR* square to a curve, a self-similar *Loft*
|xref:tutorial-origami.adoc[Miura-ori fold] |`%` for alternation, *Polyline*, *Loft* with *Straight* faces
|xref:tutorial-rose.adoc[Guilloché rose] |*Shift List* as a rotation, *Arc 3Pt* through three rings
|xref:tutorial-mashrabiya.adoc[Mashrabiya facade] |A hexagonal grid with `%`, two *Remap* from one distance, *Ruled Surface* frustums
|xref:tutorial-columns.adoc[Mushroom columns] |Several wires into one input, a scalloped rim, an array of arrays with *Linear Array*
|xref:tutorial-pendants.adoc[Pendant cloud] |A damped ripple formula, cables and orbs that move together
|xref:tutorial-enneper.adoc[Enneper surface] |A parametric surface on a (u, v) grid, *Surface From Points*, *Contour*
|xref:tutorial-stadium.adoc[Woven-bowl stadium roof] |*Arc 3Pt* through three turned rings, two wires into one *Loft*
|xref:tutorial-whirlpool.adoc[Whirlpool ziggurat] |An exact rotate-and-scale per tier, slabs by *Extrude*, corner spirals with Graft
|xref:tutorial-impeller.adoc[Impeller with tip clearance] |A lofted blade arrayed by grafted angles, a seam-safe Boolean cut, *Stack-up* and *Monte Carlo*
|xref:tutorial-morph.adoc[Helicoid–catenoid morph] |One slider through a family of minimal surfaces
|===
