= Tutorial 5: GD&T, bonus tolerance and virtual condition
:page-id: tutorial-gdt
include::_attributes.adoc[]

[.lead]
A hole of diameter .505 to .510 is located with a position tolerance of ⌀.005 at MMC. How much extra (bonus) tolerance does a hole get as it is made larger, and what are its worst-case boundaries? The numbers come from G. R. Cogorno, _Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing for Mechanical Design_ (3rd ed.), Table 3-2.

image::gdt-canvas.png[A slider for the actual size feeding the Bonus Tolerance calculator, and the boundary calculator]

== Build it

. Place a slider _Actual size: 0.508 (0.505 – 0.510)_ with Decimals `3`.
. Place a *Bonus Tolerance* (Tolerance). Wire *Actual size → A*. With menu:Set value…[], set *MMC* to `0.505`, *LMC* to `0.510` and *T* to `0.005`. Leave *Mod* at `MMC`.
. Place a *Virtual & Resultant Condition* (Tolerance). Set *MMC* `0.505`, *LMC* `0.510`, *T* `0.005`, and leave *Int* (internal) at `True`, because a hole is an internal feature.
. Place two Panels: wire *Bonus B* and *Tot* into one, and *VC*, *RC*, *IB* and *OB* into the other.

== Read the results

At an actual size of .508 the hole has departed .003 from its MMC of .505, so it gains a *bonus* of .003 and its total position tolerance is .005 + .003 = .008. Drag the slider and compare with Cogorno's table:

[cols="1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Actual size |Bonus |Total position tolerance
|.505 (MMC) |.000 |.005
|.506 |.001 |.006
|.507 |.002 |.007
|.508 |.003 |.008
|.509 |.004 |.009
|.510 (LMC) |.005 |.010
|===

The boundary calculator gives:

* *Virtual condition* .500 = MMC − tolerance: the constant boundary a mating pin must clear (the gauge pin size).
* *Resultant condition* .520 = LMC + tolerance + size tolerance: the worst-case outer boundary, which decides the minimum wall thickness around the hole.

For a hole the inner boundary is the virtual condition and the outer boundary the resultant condition; for a pin it is the other way round.

== Things to try

* Set *Mod* to `RFS` (regardless of feature size): the bonus is always zero.
* Set *Mod* to `LMC`: the tolerance now applies at the largest hole, and the bonus grows as the hole gets *smaller*.
* Model the matching pin: on the boundary calculator set *MMC* `0.510`, *LMC* `0.505` and *Int* `False`. Its virtual condition is .515 and its resultant condition .495.
* Drag the slider outside .505 – .510 by editing its range: Bonus Tolerance turns red, because an actual size outside the size limits is a rejected part, not a bonus.

See xref:tolerance.adoc#gdt[GD&T calculators] for the formulas.
