= Tutorial 32: An impeller with its tip clearance
:page-id: tutorial-impeller
include::_attributes.adoc[]

[.lead]
A turbine impeller in a cut-away shroud, with the number that matters most to its designer, the gap between blade tips and shroud, computed live as a tolerance stack-up. Twisted blades are lofted through three turned radial lines and arrayed round a hub; three *Dimension* components feed *Stack-up* and *Monte Carlo Stack-up*.

image::impeller-render.png[A grey twelve-blade impeller with a domed hub inside a copper shroud with a cut-away wedge]

Reference: choose menu:Examples[Impeller with tip clearance] on the canvas toolbar to load the finished definition. The xref:tutorial-stackup.adoc[stack-up tutorial] explains the tolerance components.

== Build it

=== One blade

. Place six sliders: _Blades: 12 (3 – 24)_ with Decimals `0`, _Hub radius: 8 (4 – 15)_, _Tip radius: 30 (15 – 50)_, _Height: 16 (6 – 30)_, _Twist: 40 (0 – 90)_, and _Nominal clearance: 0.6 (0.1 – 2)_ with Decimals `2`.
. Place two *Construct Point* (Vector) components at (*Hub radius*, 0, 0) and (*Tip radius*, 0, 0), and a *Line* (Curve) between them: the blade's root-to-tip line.
. Place two *Range* (Maths) components with *N* `2`: _Section heights_ from 0 to *Height*, and _Section turns_ from 0 to *Twist*. Each gives three values.
. Place a *Move* (Transform) with the Line *→ G* and *Section heights → Z*, then a *Rotate* (Transform) with *Section turns → A*: three lines, each higher and turned further.
. Place a *Loft* (Surface), _Blade_, with the three lines *→ C*: a twisted blade.

=== The rotor

[start=6]
. Place an *Expression* (Maths), `360/x`, with *Blades → x*, and a *Series* (Sets) with it as *N* and *Blades → C*: the blade angles.
. Place a *Rotate*, _Rotor_, with *Blade → G* and the angles *→ A*, with menu:Graft[] on *A*: one blade per angle.
. Place a *Cylinder* (Surface), _Hub_ (*Hub radius*, *Height*), and a *Sphere* (Surface) of *Hub radius* moved up by *Height*: the nose.

=== The shroud

[start=9]
. Place an *Addition* (Maths), *Tip radius* + *Nominal clearance*: the shroud's bore radius. Add `2` for the outer radius.
. Place two *Cylinder* components, outer (outer radius, *Height*) and inner (bore radius, *L* `40`, moved down by `10`), and a *Solid Difference* (Intersect): the shroud ring.
. For the cut-away, place a *Center Box* (*X*, *Y* `80`, *Z* `60`), a *Move* (*X*, *Y* `40`, *Z* `-10`) and a *Rotate* with *A* `30`, and subtract it from the ring with a second *Solid Difference*. The 30° keeps the cut off the cylinders' seams, which lie on the +X axis: a cut exactly through a seam is the case where FreeCAD's Booleans are least reliable.

=== Tip clearance

[start=12]
. Place three *Dimension* (Tolerance) components:
** _Shroud bore radius_: *N* from the bore Addition, ±0.1, direction +1.
** _Blade tip radius_: *N* from *Tip radius*, ±0.05, direction −1.
** _Hub runout_: *N* 0, ±0.05, direction −1.
. Place a *Stack-up* (Tolerance) and wire all three dimensions into its *D* (hold kbd:[Shift] for the second and third): the chain bore − tip − runout. Wire its *R* (report) into a *Panel* (Params).
. Place a *Monte Carlo Stack-up* (Tolerance) with the same three dimensions and set *LSL* to `0.3`: the minimum acceptable clearance.

== What is happening

The blade is a smooth loft through three straight lines turned 0°, 20° and 40°: its leading edge leans round as it rises, like a real compressor blade (without the airfoil thickness). Grafting the rotation angles copies the one blade round the hub.

The clearance chain reads like a sentence: the shroud bore, minus the blade tip, minus however far the hub is off-centre. With the defaults the nominal clearance is 0.6 and the worst case ±0.2, so it never drops below 0.4: the Monte Carlo finds no build under the 0.3 limit. Lower *Nominal clearance* to 0.4 and the worst case reaches 0.2, and about half a percent of random builds fall below 0.3; at 0.3 about half of them do. The geometry tests check the nominal value and the worst-case tolerance.

The rotor updates in under half a second.

== Things to try

* Drag *Nominal clearance* down from 0.6 and watch the Panel and the Monte Carlo's *%* output.
* Set *Twist* to 0 for flat, fan-like blades, or to 90 for a strongly swept rotor.
* Set *Blades* to 6 for an open propeller.
* Tighten the shroud bore's tolerance to ±0.05 and see how far the clearance can then be reduced.
