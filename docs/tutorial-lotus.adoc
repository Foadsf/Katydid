= Tutorial 35: A blooming lotus
:page-id: tutorial-lotus
include::_attributes.adoc[]

[.lead]
One cupped petal, lofted through a row of arcs, is copied round several tiers of a flower. Each tier is smaller, sits a little higher and is turned half a petal against the one outside it. A single *Bloom* slider opens the whole flower, the outer tiers furthest, so dragging it takes the flower from bud to full bloom.

image::lotus-render.png[A pink lotus with four tiers of cupped petals round a yellow-green seed pod]

Reference: choose menu:Examples[Blooming lotus] on the canvas toolbar to load the finished definition.

== Build it

=== One petal

. Place eight sliders: _Petals: 10 (5 – 16)_ and _Tiers: 4 (1 – 6)_ with Decimals `0`, _Length: 14 (6 – 25)_, _Width: 7 (2 – 12)_, _Cup: 0.35 (0 – 0.8)_ and _Curl: 0.15 (-0.3 – 0.5)_ with Decimals `2`, _Bloom: 55 (0 – 85)_, and _Taper: 0.75 (0.5 – 1)_ with Decimals `2`.
. Place a *Range* (Maths), _Along the petal_, from `0` to `0.95` with *N* `7`: eight stations from base to near the tip.
. Place an *Expression* (Maths), _Width profile_, `y*(0.35*(1 - x) + 2.2*x*(1 - x))`, with the stations *→ x* and *Width → y*: narrow at the base, widest just before the middle, almost nothing at the tip.
. Place two *Multiplication* (Maths) components: the width × *Cup* (how far the edges rise) and the stations × *Length* (the height of each station).
. Place an *Expression*, _Spine_, `y*z*x**2`, with the stations, *Curl* and *Length*: how far the petal's centre line leans out. Place another, _Edges_, `x - y`, with the spine and the cup depth: the edges sit further in.
. Place two *Multiplication* components, the width × `0.5` and × `-0.5`, and three *Construct Point* (Vector) components: left edge (edges, −half width, height), centre (spine, 0, height) and right edge (edges, +half width, height).
. Place an *Arc 3Pt* (Curve), _Sections_, through the three, and a *Loft* (Surface), _Petal_.

=== The flower

[start=8]
. Place a *Series* (Sets), _Tier no._, with *Tiers → C*, and five *Expression* components on it:
** _Tier scale_ `y**x` with *Taper → y*;
** _Tier tilt_ `y*(1 - x/z)` with *Bloom → y* and *Tiers → z*;
** _Tier radius_ `0.06*x*y*z` with the scale *→ x*, *Petals → y* and *Width → z*;
** _Tier lift_ `0.08*x*y` with *Length → y*;
** _Tier stagger_ `180*x/y` with *Petals → y*.
. Place a *Scale* (Transform) with the petal and the tier scales, a *Rotate* (Transform) with the tier tilts and *X* (axis) set to `0, 1, 0`, a *Move* (Transform) with the tier radii *→ X* and lifts *→ Z*, and a second *Rotate* with the staggers: one petal per tier, in place.
. Place an *Expression*, `360/x`, with *Petals*, a *Series* with it as *N* and *Petals → C*, and a third *Rotate*, _Flower_, with the tier petals and these angles, with menu:Graft[] on *A*: every tier copied round.
. For the seed pod, place an *Expression*, `0.048*x*y`, with *Petals* and *Width*, and a *Sphere* (Surface) of that radius.
. Turn menu:Preview[] off on the points, Sections, Petal, Scale, first Rotate, Move and second Rotate.

== What is happening

The petal is built standing up, its base at the origin, with the hollow of the cup facing −X. Rotating it about the Y axis tips it outward; moving it out along X puts its base on the tier's ring, with the hollow now facing the centre. The tier's own tilt, `Bloom × (1 − k/Tiers)`, gives the outer tier the whole Bloom angle and each inner tier less. The tier radius grows with the number and width of the petals, so they always close round the ring.

The geometry tests check, tier by tier, that each petal's base lies on its ring at its height, that the petal leans straight outward, and that it leans by exactly its share of Bloom. The flower updates in about a quarter of a second, almost all of it drawing the petals.

== Things to try

* Drag *Bloom* from 0 (a closed bud) to 85 (flat open).
* Set *Petals* to 16 and *Tiers* to 6 for a dense water lily.
* Set *Curl* to −0.3: the petal tips turn in like a tulip.
* Raise *Cup* to 0.8 for deep, spoon-shaped petals.
