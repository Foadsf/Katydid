= Tutorial 44: A continuum robot
:page-id: tutorial-continuum
include::_attributes.adoc[]

[.lead]
A tendon-driven continuum robot bends like a trunk. Drilled disks sit on a flexible backbone, and pulling the tendons that run through their eyelets curls it. In the usual constant-curvature model the backbone bends into a circular arc, and each tendon must change length by −θ·_r_·cos α: the tendon on the inside of the bend is pulled in, the one outside is let out. A *Panel* shows those changes live.

image::continuum-render.png[A curved spine of aluminium disks on a dark backbone, threaded by four red tendons, on a base]

Reference: choose menu:Examples[Continuum robot] on the canvas toolbar to load the finished definition.

== Build it

=== One vertebra

. Place nine sliders: _Disks: 14 (4 – 30)_ with Decimals `0`, _Length: 60 (20 – 120)_, _Bend: 120 (1 – 270)_, _Disk radius: 6 (3 – 12)_, _Disk thickness: 1.5 (0.5 – 4)_ with Decimals `2`, _Tendons: 4 (2 – 8)_ with Decimals `0`, _Eyelet radius: 4.5 (1.5 – 10)_ with Decimals `2`, _Routing twist: 0 (-30 – 30)_ and _Tendon radius: 0.3 (0.1 – 1)_ with Decimals `2`.
. Place a *Cylinder* (Surface) of *Disk radius* and *Disk thickness*, moved down by half the thickness (*Multiplication* × `-0.5`, *Move*): the disk, centred on z = 0.
. Place a *Circle* (Curve) of *Eyelet radius*, a *Divide Curve* (Curve) with *Tendons → N*, and a *Deconstruct* (Vector): the eyelet positions.
. Place a *Cylinder* for the holes (radius *Tendon radius* × `1.6`, length thickness + `2`), an *Expression* (Maths), `-x/2 - 1`, of the thickness, and a *Move* to the eyelets. Do the same for a central hole of radius `1`.
. Place a *Solid Difference* (Intersect), _Vertebra_, with the disk and both sets of holes wired into *B*.

=== The bend

[start=6]
. Place a *Series* (Sets), _Disk no._, with *Disks → C*, and an *Expression*, _Disk angle_, `x*y/(z - 1)`, with the disk no., *Bend* and *Disks*: from 0 to the full bend.
. Place an *Expression*, _Bend radius_, `x/radians(y)`, with *Length* and *Bend*, and a *Construct Point* (Vector) at (radius, 0, 0): the centre of curvature.
. Place a *Multiplication* of the disk no. by *Routing twist*, and a *Rotate* (Transform) of the vertebra by it, about Z: each disk turned a little more round its own axis.
. Place a *Rotate*, _Vertebrae_, with the disk angles *→ A*, the centre *→ C* and *X* (axis) `0, 1, 0`.

=== Tendons, backbone and pull

[start=10]
. Place two *Rotate* components for the eyelet points, the same two turns, with menu:Graft[] on the first one's *G*: one branch per tendon, its eyelet at every disk. Place an *Interpolate* (Curve) and a *Pipe* (Surface), _Tendons_, with *Tendon radius*.
. Place a *Construct Point* at the origin, a *Rotate* by the disk angles about the same centre, an *Interpolate* and a *Pipe*, _Backbone_, with *R* `0.8`.
. Place a *Cylinder* of *Disk radius* + `3`, `3` high, moved under the first disk: the base.
. Place a *Series*, _Tendon no._, with *Tendons*, an *Expression*, `360*x/y`, for each tendon's angle, an *Expression*, _Length change_, `-radians(x)*y*cos(radians(z))`, with *Bend*, *Eyelet radius* and the angles, and a *Panel* (Params), _Tendon pull_.
. Turn menu:Preview[] off on the construction steps.

== What is happening

Every disk is the same vertebra, turned about the centre of curvature by its share of the bend, so the disk centres lie on an arc of length *Length* and radius *Length*/θ. A tendon at angle α round the disk lies on a parallel arc whose radius is smaller by _r_·cos α, so its length is θ·(*Length*/θ − _r_ cos α) = *Length* − θ·_r_·cos α. That is the Panel's formula. With the defaults, 120° of bend and 4.5 mm eyelets, the inside tendon is pulled in by 9.4 mm and the outside one let out by the same amount. The geometry tests measure the tendon curves and compare them with the formula.

*Routing twist* turns each disk's eyelets further round, so the tendons spiral along the spine. The Panel's formula is for straight tendons and no longer applies then. The robot updates in about a third of a second.

== Things to try

* Set *Bend* to 270 to curl the robot into a hook.
* Set *Routing twist* to 15 for helically routed tendons.
* Set *Tendons* to 3 and *Disks* to 30 for a finer spine.
* Set *Bend* to 1: the robot stands straight and the Panel shows almost no change.
