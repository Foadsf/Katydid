= Tutorial 21: A flange with visible tolerance zones
:page-id: tutorial-flange
include::_attributes.adoc[]

[.lead]
A bolted flange whose GD&T you can see. Six bolt holes are located with a position tolerance at MMC. Katydid's tolerance components compute the bonus tolerance from the hole's actual size and the holes' virtual condition, and the model draws each hole's tolerance zone (exaggerated) and the gauge pin a functional gauge would use. Drag the actual hole size and watch the zones grow.

image::flange-render.png[A steel flange with green translucent gauge pins and red tolerance zones in each bolt hole]

Reference: choose menu:Examples[Flange with tolerance zones] on the canvas toolbar to load the finished definition. Read xref:tutorial-gdt.adoc[Tutorial 5] and the xref:tolerance.adoc[tolerance page] first for the ideas behind the numbers.

== The specification

The holes are ⌀9.0 to ⌀9.3 mm (MMC 9.0, LMC 9.3) with a position tolerance of ⌀0.2 at MMC. At MMC the zone is ⌀0.2; every 0.01 mm the hole is made larger adds 0.01 mm of bonus. The gauge pin that every acceptable hole must clear is the virtual condition, MMC minus the tolerance: ⌀8.8.

== Build it

=== The part

. Place seven sliders: _Flange radius: 40 (25 – 60)_, _Thickness: 8 (4 – 16)_, _Bolt circle: 28 (15 – 50)_, _Bolts: 6 (3 – 12)_ with Decimals `0`, _Actual hole Ø: 9.15 (9.0 – 9.3)_ and _Position Ø: 0.2 (0.05 – 0.5)_ with Decimals `3`, and _Zone scale: 15 (1 – 40)_ with Decimals `0`.
. Place a *Cylinder* (Surface), _Disc_, and wire *Flange radius → R* and *Thickness → L*. Place a second, _Hub_, with *R* set to `15` and *L* to `22`.
. Place a third *Cylinder*, _Bore_, with *R* `8` and *L* `30`, and a *Move* with *Z* `-2` after it so the bore cuts right through.
. Place a *Circle* (Curve) with *Bolt circle → R*, a *Divide Curve* (Curve) with *Bolts → N*, and a *Deconstruct* (Vector) after it: the X and Y of each bolt hole.
. Place an *Expression* (Maths), _Hole radius_, with *F* set to `x/2` and wire *Actual hole Ø → x*. Place an *Addition* (Maths) with *B* `2` and wire *Thickness → A*.
. Place a *Cylinder*, _Hole_ (*Hole radius → R*, *Addition → L*), and a *Move*, _Holes_ (*Hole → G*, *Deconstruct X → X*, *Y → Y*, *Z* set to `-1`).
. Place two *Solid Difference* (Intersect) components: *Disc − Holes*, then *that result − Bore*. Place a third: *Hub − Bore*.

=== The tolerance

[start=8]
. Place a *Bonus Tolerance* (Tolerance). Set value *MMC* `9.0` and *LMC* `9.3`, and wire *Actual hole Ø → A* and *Position Ø → T*. Its *Tot* is the total position tolerance at this actual size. Wire it into a *Panel* (Params) to read the number.
. Place an *Expression*, _Zone radius_, with *F* set to `x*y/2`. Wire *Tot → x* and *Zone scale → y*.
. Place a *Cylinder*, _Zone_ (*Zone radius → R*, *L* `14`), and a *Move*, _Zones_ (*Zone → G*, *Deconstruct X → X*, *Y → Y*, *Z* `-3`): one tolerance zone on each hole's axis.
. Place a *Virtual & Resultant Condition* (Tolerance). Set value *MMC* `9.0`, *LMC* `9.3`, and wire *Position Ø → T*. *VC* is the gauge pin diameter.
. Place a *Multiplication* (Maths) with *B* `0.5` and wire *VC → A*. Place a *Cylinder*, _Gauge pin_ (*Multiplication → R*, *L* `20`), and a *Move*, _Gauge pins_, at the holes with *Z* `-6`.
. Turn menu:Preview[] off on the Disc, Hub, Bore, Circle, Divide Curve, Hole, Holes, the first Solid Difference, Zone and Gauge pin.

== What is happening

At an actual size of ⌀9.15 the hole is 0.15 larger than MMC, so the total position tolerance is 0.2 + 0.15 = ⌀0.35: the Panel shows `0.35`. A real zone of ⌀0.35 would be invisible next to a ⌀9 hole, so *Zone scale* draws it 15 times larger; it is the _relative_ change that matters. Drag *Actual hole Ø* from 9.0 to 9.3 and the red zones more than double.

The green pins do not move: virtual condition is a constant boundary, the same for every acceptable hole. A hole made at MMC and placed at the edge of its ⌀0.2 zone just touches the ⌀8.8 pin; a larger hole can wander further and still clear it. That is the bonus, made visible.

The geometry tests check the flange's volume against the closed-form value and that the zone and pin radii follow Cogorno's formulas. The whole definition updates in about a tenth of a second.

== Things to try

* Drag *Actual hole Ø* from 9.0 to 9.3 and watch the Panel and the zones.
* Raise *Position Ø* to 0.4: the zones grow and the gauge pins shrink.
* Set the Bonus Tolerance's *Mod* to `RFS`: the bonus disappears and the zones stop responding to the hole size.
* Set *Bolts* to 8 and *Bolt circle* to 32.
